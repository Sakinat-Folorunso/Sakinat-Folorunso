# 👋 Hi, I'm Dr. Sakinat Folorunso

### Associate Professor of AI Systems | Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning Researcher | Educator

I am an **Associate Professor of AI Systems at Olabisi Onabanjo University (OOU), Nigeria**, working at the intersection of **Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Data Science, Natural Language Processing, and AI Education**.

My work focuses on developing **responsible, inclusive and locally relevant AI systems**, with particular interests in applications that address challenges in Africa and other underrepresented contexts.

> **Building responsible, inclusive and locally relevant AI systems through FAIR data, federated learning, language technologies and AI education.**

## 🧪 Research Themes

### 🤝 FAIR & Federated AI

Research on **FAIR data practices, federated learning, data governance and privacy-aware AI systems**, particularly for distributed and sensitive datasets.

### 🗣️ African Language AI

Exploring **Natural Language Processing, language resources and culturally grounded AI** for African languages and communities.

### 🎵 Cultural AI & Digital Heritage

Using **data science, machine learning and language technologies** to support the preservation, analysis and computational understanding of intangible cultural heritage.

### 🏥 AI for Health

Applying **machine learning, deep learning and explainable AI** to health, diagnostics and precision medicine.

### 🎓 AI Education

Designing **AI literacy frameworks, datasets, learning interventions and practical educational resources** to support educators and learners.

### 🌍 AI for Africa

Developing AI approaches that recognise the importance of **local data, local languages, cultural context, responsible data governance and inclusive participation** in AI development.
